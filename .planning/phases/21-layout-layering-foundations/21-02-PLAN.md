<objective>
Rename `dex/formatMonYear.ts` to the format-neutral `dex/formatDate.ts` and add
`formatFullDate(iso: string): string` as its sibling — one module-level
`Intl.DateTimeFormat` per format, both pinned to `timeZone: "UTC"`, both never-throw. Update the
two `formatMonYear` importers.

Purpose: FOUND-04 requires one shared UTC-safe helper. Without `timeZone: "UTC"` every show date
renders one day early for every US user — verified this research session:
`new Date("2026-08-15")` formatted in `America/New_York` yields `Aug 14, 2026`. D-31 places the
helper in the app presentation tier, not core (core has zero `Intl` usage and no display layer).

Output: `packages/app/src/dex/formatDate.ts` with two named exports, two updated importers, and
the phase's first direct unit test of this module.
</objective>
