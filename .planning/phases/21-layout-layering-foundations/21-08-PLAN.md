<objective>
Convert the FAB family onto the owner's vars and make the D-05 behavior change: the FAB's
position stops depending on whether a suggestion row is currently rendered and instead follows
the reserved-slot signal, so it moves at most once per show.

Purpose: FOUND-02 for the FAB group, plus the founding rule this milestone cites — "tap targets
never move on their own". Shipped `fabLayout.ts` takes `stripHasContent`, so the FAB jumps 112px
mid-show the instant an editor suggestion arrives, under a reaching thumb, in the dark. D-05
re-expresses the Phase-10 `a60d5e2` lift ("FAB lifted above the reserved strip") as a stable
offset rather than undoing it: the reserved slot height (112px) is always ≥ the rendered row
height, so the FAB still clears every rendered row.

Output: `showBottomFabOffset(stripSlotReserved)` composing from `--gz-fab-offset`, `FabMenu` /
`OrbitStage` / `ShowView` rewired to the reserved-slot signal, `ExploreFilterFab` converted, and
the two existing FAB tests updated.
</objective>
