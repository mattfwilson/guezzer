<objective>
Convert every full calendar date in the app to `formatFullDate` — five visible sites and the two
accessible names — and prove the display-only boundary holds.

Purpose: FOUND-04 requires one shared UTC-safe helper across every surface. D-33 adds the two
`aria-label`s because VoiceOver currently announces `2026-08-14` as a number sequence, and on the
unmark confirm that label is what identifies the show being unmarked. D-35 is load-bearing in the
other direction: the date **is** a join key in `attendance-key.ts`'s unbound branch, so a
formatted date leaking into stored data would silently break dex derivation and merge.

Output: five converted components, a new `SetlistView` render test, extended coverage on the
three existing render tests, and a regex guard on `attendanceKey` output.
</objective>
