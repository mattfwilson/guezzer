<objective>
Convert the five bottom overlays off Tailwind `bottom-16` onto the owner's chrome reserve —
deleting the now-redundant self-padding from the three that have it — and land the D-12 source
guard that makes FOUND-02's "exactly one owner" wording mechanically true.

Purpose: `bottom-16` is 64px from the **viewport** (`fixed` ignores body padding) while the tab
bar is `64px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` tall, so on an installed instance each overlay's box
overlaps the top of the tab bar by exactly one inset (D-09). RESEARCH sharpens this: only
`BingoCelebration` and `WaveToast` actually overlap in **content** —
`InstallBanner`/`UpdateToast`/`BackupToast` each set `paddingBottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`
and are accidentally flush, so converting them **must also delete that padding** or ~34px of dead
space appears inside each toast. That same padding is what makes the overlay-height store
over-reserve by one inset today; deleting it corrects the reserve in the same change.

Output: five converted overlays, a corrected overlay-inset expectation, the D-12 guard, and a
todo capturing the deferred simultaneous-overlay case.
</objective>
