## Planner decisions (RESEARCH.md Open Questions — resolved, do not re-litigate)

**OQ2 — scrim and card are SIBLINGS inside a `SheetSurface` fragment.** Three reasons, in order of
weight:
  1. **Nesting would fade the card.** `opacity` applies to the whole subtree, so a scrim parent
     animating `opacity: 0 → 1` drags the card's rendered opacity with it. `22-UI-SPEC.md`
     §Per-variant motion is explicit that the bottom-sheet **card translates with opacity
     unchanged** — nesting cannot satisfy that.
  2. `pointer-events: none` becomes a structural property of two siblings rather than an
     inheritance rule that a `pointer-events-auto` class on the card can silently defeat.
  3. It is the shape `22-RESEARCH.md` §Code Examples probed live in this repo (P1), so the whole
     close-start contract is known to hold against it.

  **Named cost, scheduled deliberately (not a regression):** `packages/app/test/layerOrder.test.tsx`'s
  case *"the shared `<Sheet>` primitive is the NEGATIVE case: same tiers, already portaled"* derives
  `const surfaceRoot = dialog.parentElement!` and asserts that parent's `zIndex` is `sheetScrim`.
  Under siblings `dialog.parentElement` is `document.body`. Task 2 amends that case. Everything in
  `sheet.a11y.test.tsx` survives unchanged — its `document.body` parentage assertions cover the five
  *hand-rolled* sheets, not `<Sheet>`.

  **Named risk, handed to the device session:** the card moves from `flex flex-col justify-end`
  inside a `fixed inset-0` parent to its own `fixed inset-x-0 bottom-0`. Overflow behaviour for a
  taller-than-viewport card is the same in both shapes (content extends upward past the viewport),
  and `SwapSheet` already bounds itself with `max-h-[70vh] overflow-y-auto` — but **`CatchUpSheet`
  bounds nothing**. Plan 22-09 carries a numbered observation for it.

**OQ4 — one motion block, `config.ui.motion`.** Seven named constants (below). `WaveToast`'s
hard-coded `transition={{ duration: 0.2 }}` is **moved** into it here (D-25 — a file this plan
touches, and `22-UI-SPEC.md` §Motion Token Contract already scopes it in). `BingoCelebration`'s twin
`0.2` moves in plan 22-08, the plan that touches that file.

**Naming decision (RESEARCH/PATTERNS disagree):** the declared bottom-overlay order is
`config.ui.BOTTOM_OVERLAY_ORDER` — the name `22-VALIDATION.md`'s CR-01 omission-guard row and
`22-RESEARCH.md` §Pattern 9 both use.

**Closed assumptions (do not re-verify):** probe P7 confirms `Maximize2`, `Minimize2` and
`Smartphone` are live exports of the installed `lucide-react@1.23.0` (A4 closed); probe P3 confirms
motion's rAF fallback runs under this repo's jsdom (A3 closed); `inert` is Baseline since Apr 2023
(A1 closed).
